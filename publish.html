YOUR LITTLE CORNER OF THE INTERNET

How to share your words.

Whenever you want to post, open the form, write what you want to share, and submit. Your post will appear on this site after GitHub refreshes it.

01

Write a journal post

Choose a topic, add a short introduction, and write your story. Technical notes, everyday moments, and half-formed ideas all belong.

OPEN STORY FORM ↗
02

Add portfolio work

Describe what you made, choose a category, and add an optional project link or image.

OPEN PORTFOLIO FORM ↗
03

Publish it

Press Submit new issue at the bottom of GitHub’s form. That’s it. Your entry becomes public and shows up here automatically.

It can take a few minutes for the page to update. You do not need to edit files or touch code.

That’s all there is to it .

Every entry you send is public on GitHub and on this website. To edit or remove a post later, open its GitHub issue and make the change there.
